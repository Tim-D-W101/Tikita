The house

Aloha 16 is let as a whole house, so the pool, the deck and the beach are yours and your group's for the week. It suits big families and groups of friends who want to cook, braai and spend the day between the pool and the sea.

  • Rim-flow pool
  • Beach access
  • Braai area
  • Full kitchen
  • Air conditioning
  • Outdoor showers
  • Wi-Fi
  • Two garages
Bedrooms
Five bedrooms, each a separate pod set apart from the living area, with its own en-suite bathroom, air conditioning and a private outdoor shower. Bedrooms 1 to 3 have extra-length king beds. Bedrooms 4 and 5 each have a queen bed and two bunk beds.
Cottage
Two single beds, its own bathroom and air conditioning.
Living
The kitchen, dining room and lounge are one open-plan space that opens straight onto the wooden deck.
Deck and pool
A rim-flow pool on the deck, with the beach in front of the house.
Kitchen
Gas hob, a large oven, two fridges and two chest freezers, so there's room for a week's shopping.
Braai
A big braai area with a table, built-in benches and sand underfoot. Braai tools and charcoal are provided.
Also
Wi-Fi, two garages and parking, and housekeepers who look after the house during your stay.

Getting here

Aloha 16 is in Ponta Mamoli, north of Ponta Malongane. Allow about an hour from the Kosi Bay border. You'll need a 4x4: the last stretch is sand, and two-wheel drives can't get onto the estate.

  • Shop before the border. There's a large Boxer and Spar in Manguzi. Malongane has the basics: fresh produce, local bread, beer, wine and spirits.
  • Let your tyres down to about 1.2 to 1.5 bar before the sand.
  • Save these directions before you leave the tar, or print them.

Good to know

Malaria

Ponta Mamoli is a very low-risk malaria area, and every room has mosquito spray. If you'd like to take preventatives, ask your pharmacist before you travel.

The pool and the sea

The rim-flow pool has no fence and no cover, and there are no lifeguards at the pool or on Ponta Mamoli beach. Children need an adult with them at all times.

House rules

  • No pets.
  • No parties, functions or loud music. The estate is quiet from 22:30.
  • No wood fires.
  • Only the guests on your booking may stay. Anyone extra will be asked to leave.
